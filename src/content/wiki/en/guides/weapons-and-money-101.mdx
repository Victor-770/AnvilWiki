## What is the upgrade order that actually works?

The shop sells everything from day one, which is exactly how it gets you. Buying in the wrong order is the difference between cruising through Island 3 and rebuilding your bankroll from sardines. The community-tested order:

1. **Bright spinner lure** — cheapest bite-rate multiplier in the game. Buy before anything else.
2. **Second grill slot** — food security is DPS security.
3. **Shotgun** — the Swarm's leech waves are unmanageable with the starter pistol alone.
4. **Feather rig lure** — unlocks flying fish, which is where trick-shot money lives.
5. **SMG** — sustained fire for swarms; the shotgun's reload will get someone killed.
6. **Assault rifle + deep troll rig** — the Island 3/4 combo for dueling big fish.
7. **Boat engine** — only after the arsenal; speed is a luxury until the map gets long.
8. **Everything else** — cosmetics, the fourth grill, the slot cabinet skin. Flex responsibly.

The long version, with prices and the boring-drawer items, is in the [Arsenal & Lures guide](/guides/arsenal-and-lures/).

## How do trick-shot payouts work?

Any catch landed while airborne pays **5× its base value** if you tag it with a weapon mid-air. The intended target is flying fish with the feather rig: cast low, spook the school, and shoot on the rise. A good gunner can clear 300+ chips per minute on a settled school — more than rod fishing, less patience required.

- **The 360** pays the same as the plain shot — the style points are entirely emotional. Do it anyway.
- **Dynamite does NOT qualify** for trick-shot multipliers. Explosions are their own reward.
- Missed shots spook the school for about ten seconds. Two misses per school is the break-even for patience.

<Callout type="tip" title="Assign roles early">
Assign the gunner role **before** the school spawns, not during. Friendly-fire payouts are negative. The four-role split is in the [co-op crew roles guide](/guides/co-op-crew-roles/).
</Callout>

## What are the casino rules worth keeping?

The fish casino is a chip sink wearing a slot cabinet costume — except for two genuinely profitable interactions. Everything else is entertainment billed as strategy:

- **The high-roller table pays a drip premium** on rare species (urchins and up). Selling rare fish here instead of the dock is free money — this is the one casino interaction that is always correct.
- **Charms** bought with chips apply a +10% payout stack that persists for the session. One charm is an investment; five charms is a story for later.
- **The slot cabinet** has a published-ish house edge and a jackpot animation designed to be screenshotted. Set a chip budget, enjoy the lights, walk away.

<Callout type="warn" title="Chips are one-way">
Casino chips do **not** convert back to dollars. Every chip you spin is money that will never buy a grill slot again. The house thanks you for your service.
</Callout>

## When should you break these rules?

On **Hard difficulty** the payout curve shifts (roughly +40%) and swarm pressure arrives a day earlier, which moves the shotgun up to slot two and makes charms nearly mandatory before Island 3. On **Easy**, you can skip the SMG entirely and reach the Golden Isle on shotgun momentum alone. Rules are load-bearing until the lobby settings change — then re-read this page and buy the shotgun first.
