## Which How to Fish patch changed the game the most?

v1.0.9 (August 2026) — difficulty selection. Easy and Hard modes at lobby creation change AI aggression, hunger drain and the payout curve, and Hard mode carries its own Island 5 achievement. If you only learn two patches, make it v1.0.9 (changes your shopping list) and v1.0.6 (changes how you stop playing). Every patch in plain language, newest first, below.

## Patch 1.0.9 · Difficulty selection (Aug 2026)

- New Easy and Hard modes at lobby creation — AI aggression and hunger drain scale accordingly.
- Hard mode payouts +40% and a dedicated achievement for clearing Island 5 on it.
- Fixed fish despawning under piers when the boat parked too close.

What it means: the [upgrade order](/guides/weapons-and-money-101/) now has a Hard variant — shotgun moves up to slot two, and charms become near-mandatory before Island 3.

## Patch 1.0.8 · Boss tuning pass (Jul 2026)

- Pufferfish Prime phase 2 inflation attack now telegraphs 0.4s longer.
- Boss trophy payout rebalanced across all difficulties.
- New 4K golden slot cabinet skin for casino regulars.

What it means: the inflation windows in the [Pufferfish Prime guide](/bosses/pufferfish-prime/) are more forgiving than the old clips suggest — trust the spikes, not your panic. Trophy rebalance also touched the [high-roller table math](/guides/casino-economics/).

## Patch 1.0.7 · The harpoon update (Jun 2026)

- Harpoon gun added — pins large fish for the reel, one charge per voyage.
- Reef Tyrant fight rebalanced around pin mechanics.
- Grill smoke now visually drifts downwind (pure vibes, zero gameplay).

What it means: the harpoon slot in the [arsenal upgrade order](/guides/arsenal-and-lures/) is now a boss utility line item, and the [Reef Tyrant](/bosses/reef-tyrant/) is a gear check for it specifically.

## Patch 1.0.6 · Save behavior rework (Jun 2026)

- Quitting to the menu now forces a save write before shutdown.
- Alt+F4 at sea still skips the save — the game tells you so, loudly.
- Cloud sync conflict dialog now shows timestamps for both saves.

What it means: the save-conflict recovery steps in the [Fix-It Desk](/fixes/fix-it-desk/) got safer — the newer-timestamp rule now works because you can actually see both timestamps.

## Patch 1.0.5 · Social patch (Jun 2026)

- Lobbies raised to 8 anglers; crew roles persist between sessions.
- In-game chat wheel added (yes, "FISH ON!" is on it).
- Reduced background GPU load on calm-water scenes.

What it means: 8/8 lobbies are also the least stable per community reports — the [lobby troubleshooting section](/fixes/fix-it-desk/) covers the visibility and region fixes.

## Patch 1.0.4 · Co-op groundwork (May 2026)

- First pass at larger lobby sizes and role assignment.
- Seagull raid timing fixed to pause while players are in menus.
- Accessibility: colorblind palettes for lure rarity.

What it means: the [crew roles](/guides/co-op-crew-roles/) became persistent between sessions — set them once, and the seagull wars got slightly more polite.
