## What are the four co-op roles in How to Fish?

A crew that assigns roles before the first cast stops tripping over itself by Island 2. Each role owns one system on the boat — food, fish, guns or money — and every boss fight in the game tests at least one of them. Since patch 1.0.5 roles persist between sessions, so set them once and let the muscle memory build.

## 🧑‍🍳 The Chef — owns the grill and the grocery run

The least glamorous job and the one that decides the most voyages.

- Keeps the hunger bar of the whole crew fed
- Fillets & grills during boss downtime
- Guards the stove from seagull heists

The full kitchen system — recipes, batch rotation and the seagull counters — is in the [cooking & recipes guide](/guides/cooking-and-recipes/).

## 🎣 The Angler — keeps the lines wet, always

The money maker, and the role that turns the fishipedia into income.

- Matches lures to the species table
- Farms flying fish for trick-shot payouts
- Reports hotspot zones to the crew

Which lure gets which bite is the [fishipedia's](/fish/fishipedia-species-database/) whole job; the night-cycle windows are in [Survival 101](/guides/survival-101/).

## 🔫 The Gunner — crowd control with a shotgun

Every swarm wave and every mid-air payout runs through this role.

- Deletes leech swarms before they board
- Lands 360 trick shots for 5× payouts
- Peels predators off the Chef

The trick-shot economy (and why dynamite never qualifies) is in [Weapons & Money 101](/guides/weapons-and-money-101/); the swarm answers live in the [arsenal guide](/guides/arsenal-and-lures/).

## 🧭 The Captain — drives, banks and decides

The role that says "no" so the crew can afford to say "yes" later.

- Holds the purse & plans upgrades
- Picks routes between hotspot zones
- Calls the retreat when dynamite is out

<Callout type="tip" title="The purse is load-bearing">
Chips never convert back to dollars — the Captain budgets them like they are already gone. The full spending discipline is in [Casino Economics](/guides/casino-economics/), and every boss's retreat point is marked in the [boss guides](/bosses/).
</Callout>

## Crew tips from a thousand wipes

Six habits that separate crews who clear Island 5 from crews who fund it:

- 🍳 **Chef:** The grill outranks the gun. A second grill slot pays for itself within a day — your second SMG never will.
- 🎣 **Angler:** Cast in neutral. Prop noise scatters schools faster than dynamite does, and dynamite is at least fun.
- 🎯 **Gunner:** Two misses per school, then move. Patience is a resource with a price tag.
- 🧭 **Captain:** Chips never convert back to dollars. Budget them like they are already gone — because they are.
- 📦 **Chef:** One cooked stack in the pantry, always. Hunger does not schedule itself around boss fights.
- 🌙 **Angler:** Night is not downtime. It is when the expensive fish forget the boat exists.

Solo player? You're all four at once — the [achievement hunt](/guides/achievement-hunt/) notes which checks (Lone Wave, Deckhand) assume a crew, and the [island walkthrough](/guides/island-walkthrough/) marks where a 3–4 person lobby makes the biggest difference.
