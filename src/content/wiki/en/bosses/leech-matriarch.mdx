## How do you fight the Leech Matriarch?

She only spawns if your crew dawdles on wave-three cleanup — board the objective fast and she never appears; linger and the water goes dark. Two phases: **Brood Guard**, where leech drones heal her faster than you damage her, and **Boarding Run**, where she charges the hull and steals your cooked food. Drone-first targeting wins phase one, and a pistol shot interrupts every charge in phase two.

## What is the spawn condition?

The Matriarch is a punishment for slow objective cleanup on Island 2. The [island walkthrough](/guides/island-walkthrough/) maps the three boarding waves; if your crew clears wave three's objective promptly, she never surfaces at all. Lingering to farm the post-wave bite window is exactly how you meet her.

## Phase 1 — Brood Guard

She sits under the wreck while leech drones heal her faster than you damage her. Killing drones first is the whole fight: assign one gunner purely to spawns while the angler baits her to the surface with a meat chunk.

<Callout type="tip" title="The bait window">
Meat chunk aggro is the angler's contribution — the same lure that fixes [post-swarm dry bites](/fish/fishipedia-species-database/) also drags her into shooting range.
</Callout>

## Phase 2 — Boarding Run

Below half health she charges the hull. Every charge that connects steals a cooked pantry stack — the fight literally eats your dinner. The counter is embarrassingly simple: anyone with a pistol can shoot the charge mid-water and turn her away.

Keep one crew member on pistol duty for the whole phase, even if they were reeling a moment ago. The pantry stack she steals is the same "one cooked stack, always" parachute from [Survival 101](/guides/survival-101/) — losing it mid-fight is how the hunger drift starts.

<Callout type="tip" title="Loot — cook it, never sell it">
She drops the **Matriarch Fillet** — a recipe ingredient that makes the best rations in the game. Cook it; do not sell it. Future-you on Island 3 says thanks.
</Callout>

## How does she compare to the other bosses?

She's the coordination check in the four-fight gauntlet: the [Pufferfish Prime](/bosses/pufferfish-prime/) tests dynamite discipline, the [Reef Tyrant](/bosses/reef-tyrant/) tests gear order, and the [Golden Leviathan](/bosses/golden-leviathan/) tests greed. Hers is the only fight that punishes standing still — which, on a fishing boat, is a fair lesson to teach early.
