## What is in the How to Fish fishipedia?

Every species the community has catalogued across the five islands — 28 catchable creatures and two boss fights — with the lure pairings that actually get bites. Values are ballpark chips before casino multipliers. Three rules explain most of the list: night species only bite in the dark hours, skittish species despawn when the boat is loud, and rare species are worth more at the back-room scale than the dock.

## Which three rules make the whole list click?

<Callout type="tip" title="The drip premium">
Rare species (urchin tier and up) sell for noticeably more at the **high-roller table** inside the casino than at the dock. Boss trophies count as rare — haul them to the chips window, never the dock scale. The full math is in [Casino Economics](/guides/casino-economics/).
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Night shifts matter">
Glow-lure species only bite during the dark hours of each island's cycle. A crew that sleeps through the night catches half the fishipedia — the expensive half. The rotation habits are in [Survival 101](/guides/survival-101/).
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="The quiet rule">
Skittish species (ghost marlin, comet tuna) despawn when the boat is loud. Engine to neutral, radio off, and assign one crew to just… be calm — the [crew roles guide](/guides/co-op-crew-roles/) suggests it's usually the Angler's call.
</Callout>

Missing a species for **Fishipedia Complete**? Check the lure column against your tackle box first — most gaps are one [shop purchase](/guides/arsenal-and-lures/) away, not one lucky cast.
